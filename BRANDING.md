# Griots Books — Brand Identity

This identity is based on the owner-supplied Griots Books logo, featuring the storyteller, book, quill, and the motto **Stories. Memory. Legacy.**

## Brand palette
- Espresso / primary ink: `#22170D`
- Deep heritage gold / accessible accent: `#946000`
- Bright illustrative gold: `#D69B18`
- Warm ivory: `#F7F3EE`
- Soft parchment: `#F1E8DC`

The logo file at `public/brand/griots-books-logo.webp` is an optimized copy of the supplied logo intended for web navigation and preview. Replace it with a higher-resolution owner-approved asset for large printed artwork. Preserve the original composition and motto.

The shared brand signature component (`src/components/BrandSignature.tsx`) is used in the public and portal navigation. The full supplied logo appears in the public footer and on the homepage hero. The favicon is a small, coordinated G monogram designed for legibility in browser tabs.

Publication, editorial, and academic workflows are unchanged by this branding update.
